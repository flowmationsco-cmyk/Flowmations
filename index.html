About Flowmations

Flowmations is an automation studio in Eugene, Oregon, founded by Ryder Bianchini and Eli Whittaker. It started with a simple observation: local businesses lose real money to busy work that has an easy fix — calls that ring out, quotes nobody chases, invoices that sit unpaid for weeks.

We build the systems that fix it, and we keep them running. We’re a small team by design: you talk directly to the people who build your automations, and your business is never ticket #4,872.

We work with local businesses in Eugene and around Oregon first — salons, trades, studios, restaurants and more. If you’re further out and want the busy work gone, we’re listening.

Questions, answered

Will this mess with the software I already use?

No. We build around your existing tools — your phone system, scheduler, and invoicing stay exactly as they are. Our systems connect to them; they don’t replace them.

How long does setup take?

Most systems are live within one to two weeks of the kickoff call. You’ll see the first recovered lead before the first invoice.

What if something breaks?

Your monthly plan covers monitoring and fixes — we usually know something’s off before you do, and it’s our job to fix it, not yours.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can read a text message, you can use everything we build. Setup happens on our side.

How fast does it pay for itself?

Growth is $300 a month. If your average visit or job is worth a few hundred dollars, one or two recovered customers a month cover it — the calculator above does the napkin math with your own numbers. Everything past that is money you were leaving on the table.

Is there a contract?

Month-to-month after setup. Your founding-client rate stays locked for as long as you stay — leave whenever you want, though we don’t think you’ll want to.

How do I pick my 3 systems on the Growth plan?

We recommend based on where your audit shows the biggest leak. Most companies start with Missed-Call Recovery, Review Requests, and Lead Follow-Up.
